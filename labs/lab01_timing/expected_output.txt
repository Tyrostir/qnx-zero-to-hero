Lab 01.2 — expected output shape
================================

[UNVERIFIED] — this file records what a CORRECT run should look like.
It will be replaced with real observed output once block V6 is run.

Your numbers will differ. What must hold is the SHAPE:

  * min   >= 1000 us      (nanosleep guarantees AT LEAST the request)
  * mean  slightly above 1000 us
  * max   noticeably higher than mean -- often 2x or more
  * jitter = max - min


Illustrative run on an idle target
----------------------------------

samples : 10000   requested interval : 1000 us
min     :  1000 us
mean    :  1043 us
p50     :  1032 us
p99     :  1180 us
max     :  2350 us
jitter  :  1350 us  (max - min)

Note: worst case is more than 2x the request. See Chapter 01 section 2.3.


How to read it
--------------

mean 1043 us   <- the datasheet number. A throughput statistic.
max  2350 us   <- the only number a deadline can be built on.

A task that meets a 1100 us deadline 99% of the time fails a HARD
requirement 100% of the time.

Why even a good result overshoots: QNX's default clock tick is 1 ms, so a
1 ms sleep almost always wakes on the NEXT tick. Timer resolution is itself
a source of jitter, and it is adjustable -- see Chapter 14 (ClockPeriod).
